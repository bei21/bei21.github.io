My research focuses on **Multimodal AI**, **Document Understanding**, and **AI Agents**.
I study agents that find evidence, reason across documents, and carry out professional work.
Recent projects include [OfficeTown](#wei2026officetown), [DocAtlas](#wei2026docatlas), and [XL-DocBench](#wei2026xldocbench), part of [Office Intelligence](https://officeintelligence.github.io/).

I am also a Guest Associate Professor at Nagoya University, Japan.
I earned my Ph.D. and Master’s degrees at Kyoto University, advised by Professors Katsumi Tanaka, Masatoshi Yoshikawa, and Makoto P. Kato, and my Bachelor’s at Nanjing University, China.

I welcome research collaborations, academic visits, and internship inquiries on multimodal agents.
